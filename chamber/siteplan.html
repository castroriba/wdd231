W05 Project Site Plan: Kampala Eats

A blueprint for the Individual Website Project.

Site Name (Criterion 1)

Name: Kampala Eats (KLA Eats)

Reason: The name is brief, memorable, and directly establishes the site's geographic focus (Kampala) and content area (food reviews), which is essential for branding and recognition.

Optional Domain: kampala-eats.com

Site Purpose (Criterion 2)

The primary purpose of **Kampala Eats** is to serve as a comprehensive, curated hub for food exploration in Kampala. It will provide **independent, detailed reviews** and **location information** for local restaurants and street food vendors, helping both residents and visitors discover high-quality dining experiences. The site's content will include:

  • Detailed restaurant and vendor profiles (dynamic content from JSON).
  • Interactive map view showing locations.
  • A simple contact/suggestion form.

Scenarios (Target Audience) (Criterion 3)

These questions reflect the target audience (food enthusiasts, tourists, and locals):

  1. **"I'm visiting the city center today. What are the top 3 highly-rated street food vendors that are open right now?"** (Drives content filtering, hours of operation, and rating displays.)
  2. **"I need to find a moderately priced restaurant that serves traditional Ugandan cuisine and is within 5 kilometers of my current location."** (Drives map/location features, price filtering, and cuisine categorization.)

Color Scheme (Criterion 4 & 5)

The scheme uses colors inspired by the Ugandan flag and local street signs for high visibility and cultural relevance.

  • Primary (Accent): #D52B1E (Red). Used for headings, CTA buttons, active links, and prominent borders.
  • Secondary (Highlight): #FFD700 (Gold). Used for navigation hover states, section dividers, and subtle accents.
  • Background: #F7F7F7 (Light Gray). Used for main body background.
  • Text: #333333 (Dark Gray). Used for all body text for contrast.

(Note: These colors are applied to this document's headings, background, and text via the embedded CSS.)

Typography (Criterion 6)

The fonts are selected for high readability and a modern, bold look.

  • Headings (`h1`, `h2`): **Oswald**. A bold, condensed sans-serif font used for maximum impact and visibility. (Used for headings in this document.)
  • Body Text & Navigation: **Open Sans**. A highly readable and accessible sans-serif font suitable for long-form content and UI elements. (Used for body text in this document.)

Wireframes: Home Page Layout (Criterion 7)

The home page will feature the latest content (reviews) and primary navigation.

Mobile View (Small Screen)

Layout is a single column stack: Logo/Hamburger Menu, Hero Image, Main CTA, Latest 3 Reviews (stacked), Footer.

Desktop View (Wider Viewport)

Layout features: Logo/Horizontal Nav, Large Hero, Main Content Area (Latest Reviews) in a two-column grid, Sidebar for Filters/Ad, Footer.
